\section{Setup and claims}
Device phonon Green's functions on the one-sided grid $\omega\ge 0$,
$\hbar\equiv 1$ units inside traces, solver (occupation-positive) sign
convention $-i G^{\lessgtr}\succeq 0$:
\begin{align}
  G^R(\omega) &= \bigl[z^2(\omega) - D - \Sigma^R_{\rm tot}(\omega)\bigr]^{-1},
  \qquad z^2 = \omega^2 + 2i\eta\omega, \\
  G^{\lessgtr}(\omega) &= G^R\,\Sigma^{\lessgtr}_{\rm tot}\,G^A,
  \qquad G^A=(G^R)^\dagger,\qquad
  \Sigma_{\rm tot} = \Sigma_L + \Sigma_R + \Sigma_s .
\end{align}
The 3-phonon scattering self-energy is the Fock/bubble functional of $G$
with the device third-order force-constant (FC3) vertex
$\Phi_{ijk}=\partial^3 E/\partial u_i\partial u_j\partial u_k$
(plain truncation to device degrees of freedom),
\begin{equation}
  \Sigma_s^{\lessgtr}[G](\omega)\;\sim\;\sum \Phi\, G^{\lessgtr}G^{\lessgtr}\,\Phi
  \quad\text{(frequency convolution, bosonic fold } G^<_{ij}(-\omega)=G^>_{ji}(\omega)).
\end{equation}
The energy current into the device from terminal $a\in\{L,R,s\}$ is
\begin{equation}
  J_a=\sum_{\omega\ge 0}\hbar\omega\,
  \Tr\!\bigl[\Sigma_a^<(\omega)G^>(\omega)-\Sigma_a^>(\omega)G^<(\omega)\bigr].
  \label{eq:mw}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Claim A (the divergence identity and its two cancellation routes).}
Inserting the Keldysh products, the pointwise divergence is the
\emph{ordering commutator}
\begin{equation}
  D(\omega)\equiv\Tr\!\bigl[\Sigma^<_{\rm tot}G^>-\Sigma^>_{\rm tot}G^<\bigr]
  = \Tr\!\bigl[\Sigma^<_{\rm tot}\bigl(G^R\Sigma^>_{\rm tot}G^A
  - G^A\Sigma^>_{\rm tot}G^R\bigr)\bigr].
  \label{eq:pointwise}
\end{equation}
It vanishes through either of two distinct mechanisms:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Keldysh route ($\eta=0$):} with
$\Sigma^R-\Sigma^A=\Sigma^>-\Sigma^<$ (the half rule satisfies this
exactly; a Hermitian KK part cancels in the difference) and
$z^2-(z^2)^*=4i\eta\omega=0$, one has $G^>-G^<=G^R-G^A$ and both
resolvent orderings $G^R\Delta\Sigma G^A=G^A\Delta\Sigma G^R$, so
$D(\omega)=0$ for any (even non-reciprocal) data.
\item \textbf{Reciprocity route (any $\eta$):} if all matrices are
complex-symmetric, $M=M^T$ (phonon reciprocity), the transpose trick
$\Tr M=\Tr M^T$ maps one trace ordering into the other and
$D(\omega)=0$ identically.
\end{itemize}
At finite $\eta$ \emph{and} broken reciprocity, $D(\omega)\neq0$:
this term \emph{is} the precise form of the $\eta$-broadening
absorption. The sum rule
\begin{equation}
  J_L+J_R+J_s=-\sum_{\omega\ge0}\hbar\omega\,D(\omega)
  \label{eq:sumrule}
\end{equation}
then carries an $\eta$-commutator budget; the lead balance measures
$|J_s + \sum\hbar\omega D|$.

\paragraph{Claim B (the FC3 symmetry enters here).}
$J_s$ itself vanishes \emph{only} when $\Sigma_s=F[G]$ is evaluated with the
\emph{same} $G$ on all legs and the vertex is \emph{totally symmetric}
under the permutation group $S_3$,
$\Phi_{ijk}=\Phi_{\sigma(i)\sigma(j)\sigma(k)}\;\forall\sigma\in S_3$:
the $\hbar\omega$-weighted triad sums of the bubble telescope pairwise
(L\"u \& Wang, PRB \textbf{76}, 165418 (2007), App.~B; the cubic vertex is
``symmetric with respect to simultaneous permutations'' --- Wang, Wang \&
L\"u, EPJ~B \textbf{62}, 381 (2008); the device vertex is a third
derivative, hence $S_3$, Luisier, PRB \textbf{86}, 245407 (2012)).
A vertex that is not totally symmetric is not the third derivative of any
potential; the theory is then not $\Phi$-derivable and $J_s\neq 0$.

\paragraph{Corollary.}
At an SCBA fixed point with an exactly $S_3$ vertex,
$J_s=P_{\rm in}-P_{\rm out}=0$ and the lead currents balance,
$J_L=-J_R$, \emph{at any} $\eta$. Away from the fixed point
(mixed-iterate $\Sigma\neq F[G]$), $J_s\neq0$ and the lead balance is
finite --- it is a \emph{convergence} diagnostic, not an error of the
formulation.

\textbf{The floor is exactly the finite-$\eta$ ordering-commutator term.}
The toy table of \S2.1 shows when it vanishes: at $\eta=0$ (any data), or
at any $\eta$ when all matrices are complex-symmetric (reciprocity, the
transpose trick). Production breaks reciprocity \emph{physically}: under a
temperature bias the partial combination
$n_LG^R\gamma_LG^A+n_RG^R\gamma_RG^A$ is non-reciprocal (only the
equilibrium combination symmetrizes via the resolvent identity), measured
$|G^<-G^{<T}|/|G^<|=2\!-\!5\times10^{-2}$ in the current-carrying window
\emph{already ballistically}; the bubble inherits it
($\Sigma_s$ asymmetry $0.5\!-\!4\times10^{-2}$), and
$\eta\times{\rm asym}$ survives in the commutator. Ballistic transport
still conserves at $3\times10^{-15}$ because there
$\Sigma^{\lessgtr}_{\rm tot}$ (leads only) and $G^R$ \emph{are} exactly
complex-symmetric. The $\eta$-dependence is non-monotonic (floors
$3.7/6.9/3.2\times10^{-6}$ at $\eta=0.225/0.45/0.9$): larger $\eta$ also
smooths the nonequilibrium asymmetry. The d5a floor
($2\times10^{-2}$ at 100\,K) is the same term, amplified by soft
carriers ($\eta/\omega$ large where the current flows) and the larger
$\Sigma_s$.

\paragraph{Consequences.}
(i) The FC3/S3 chain is fully exonerated: every step verified to machine
precision, and $J_s$ at the fixed point is $10^{-8}$-relative.
(ii) The lead balance does NOT converge to zero at finite $\eta$ under
bias --- it converges to the commutator term. The correct convergence
gates are the $\Sigma$-residual and the (pairing-exact) bubble balance;
the lead balance carries an irreducible, now \emph{computable}
$\eta$-budget (the commutator term can be evaluated per run and
subtracted, giving an $\eta$-compensated balance that does go to zero).
(iii) Side finding: the RGF \texttt{selected\_solve} retarded output is
the \emph{left-connected} $g^R$ except at the last diagonal block --- do
not use it as the physical $G^R$ (the phonon observables never do; only
diagnostics were affected).

\section{Mechanism, fixes, literature (2026-06-13)}

\subsection{Fluctuation--dissipation imbalance (``damping without fluctuation'')}
The broadening of the propagator is
$i[(G^R)^{-\dagger}-(G^R)^{-1}]=\Gamma_{\rm leads}+4\eta\omega\,\mathbb{1}$:
$\eta$ adds a damping channel $\Gamma_\eta=4\eta\omega$ to $G^R$ with NO
companion fluctuation $\Sigma_\eta^{\lessgtr}$. A conserving (Baym--Kadanoff /
$\Phi$-derivable) theory requires every dissipative self-energy to carry a
matching $\Sigma^{\lessgtr}$ (Kadanoff--Baym / fluctuation--dissipation
balance). $\eta$ is thus a ``ghost reservoir'' at undefined occupation: in
equilibrium or in a reciprocal system its in/out flows cancel; under bias
(non-reciprocal $G$) it carries a net spurious energy current
$=\sum\hbar\omega D(\omega)$. Toy proof (\texttt{eta\_mechanism.py},
\texttt{probe\_fix.py}): two biased leads on a harmonic chain conserve to
$10^{-16}$ at \emph{any} $\eta$ while reciprocal; a controlled
complex-antisymmetric component $\epsilon$ in $\Sigma_s$ turns on a lead
imbalance scaling as $\eta\times\epsilon$ (zero if either is zero).

\subsection{The production non-reciprocity is the bubble's complex-asymmetry}
On the converged cnt33 fixed point, complex-symmetrizing
$\Sigma_s^{\{R,<,>\}}\to\tfrac12(\Sigma_s+\Sigma_s^T)$ collapses the defect
$1.9\times10^{-4}\to2.4\times10^{-13}$ (eight orders; \texttt{sym\_test.py}).
The bubble carries $\mathrm{asym}(\Sigma_s^R)=2.8\%$,
$\mathrm{asym}(\Sigma_s^<)=3.5\%$; phonon reciprocity demands the exact
$\Sigma_s^R$ be complex-symmetric, so that part is a numerical artifact, while
$\mathrm{asym}(\Sigma_s^<)$ has a physical nonequilibrium part from
$\mathrm{asym}(G^<)$. The defect needs $\Sigma^<,\Sigma^>,G^R$ all symmetric
(one sub-block alone does not help). \textbf{Crucially, symmetrizing shifts the
lead heat current by $+1.5\%$}: the tiny lead balance does not certify a
$10^{-6}$-accurate conductance --- conservation is necessary but not
sufficient for accuracy.

\subsection{Three levers}
(1) \textbf{Grid refinement} cuts the numerical asymmetry: the d5a floor
halved $2.06\to1.09\times10^{-2}$ from 101 to 201 points at $\eta=0.11$.
(2) \textbf{$\eta\to0$ extrapolation} removes both the floor ($\propto\eta$)
and the conductance systematic; limited by SCBA stability for soft modes (d5a
does not converge below $\eta\approx0.11$). (3) \textbf{B\"uttiker dephasing
probe} on the $\eta$-channel: $\Sigma_\eta^<=i\Gamma_\eta n_p$,
$\Sigma_\eta^>=i\Gamma_\eta(n_p+1)$ with self-consistent
$n_p=G^</(G^>-G^<)$ (zero local current per energy) drives the toy defect
$6$--$10\%\to10^{-16}$ at finite $\eta$ without touching $\Sigma_s$
(\texttt{probe\_fix.py}) --- but it injects elastic dephasing (physics), so it
is for a wanted incoherent channel, not a pure regulator.
\subsection{Quantified: the conductance ratio needs $\eta\to0$ extrapolation}
cnt33 L2, 300\,K, ballistic and anharmonic at \emph{matched} $\eta$
(\texttt{ratio\_eta.py}; the earlier ``ratio $>1$'' was a stale-ballistic
$\eta$-mismatch bug, now fixed in \texttt{transport.py}):
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{cccccc}
\toprule
$\eta$ & 0.30 & 0.45 & 0.60 & 0.90 & 0.45 (241 pt)\\
$G_{\rm anh}/G_{\rm ball}$ & 0.793 & 0.838 & 0.872 & 0.920 & 0.805\\
lead bal & 5.1e\text-6 & 6.9e\text-6 & 7.3e\text-6 & 3.2e\text-6 & 3.3e\text-6\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
The lead balance stays $\sim\!10^{-5}$ throughout, but the \emph{ratio} swings
$0.79\!\to\!0.92$: a $\sim\!13$-point finite-$\eta$ systematic, far larger than
the lead balance suggests. Extrapolating $\eta\to0$ (quadratic 0.68, linear
0.70--0.74) gives $G_{\rm anh}/G_{\rm ball}(\eta\!\to\!0)\approx0.70$ --- a
$\sim\!30\%$ anharmonic reduction, \emph{double} the $\sim\!16\%$ read off at
the single point $\eta=0.45$. Refining the grid at fixed $\eta=0.45$
($0.838\!\to\!0.805$) moves the ratio the same way (toward $\eta\to0$). See
\texttt{phonon/studies/out/fig/cnt33\_ratio\_eta\_extrapolation.pdf}.
\textbf{Implication: a single-$\eta$ production number underestimates the
anharmonic effect by $\sim$2$\times$; transport ratios must be
$\eta\to0$-extrapolated (or at least quoted with the finite-$\eta$ band).}


\subsection{Literature (Scite-verified, no retractions)}
Bare self-energies break $I_L+I_R=0$ whereas SCBA conserves (Wang, Wang \&
L\"u, EPJ B \textbf{62}, 381, 2008); approximate-$\Sigma$ substitution
``generically results in a spurious violation of current conservation''
(Klein \& Michaeli, JPCL \textbf{14}, 897, 2023); conservation
$\Leftrightarrow$ $\Phi$-derivability with consistent $G^{R},G^{\lessgtr}$
(Thygesen \& Rubio, PRB \textbf{77}, 115333, 2008; Mera et al., PRB
\textbf{86}, 161404, 2012). The self-consistent B\"uttiker/temperature probe
is already in atomistic phonon NEGF --- probe temperatures solved ``until the
integrated energy current vanishes for each B\"uttiker probe'' (Miao et al.,
APL \textbf{108}, 113107, 2016); harmonic-chain self-consistent reservoirs
verify leakage $<10^{-12}$ (Bandyopadhyay \& Segal, PRE \textbf{84}, 011151,
2011; Roy \& Dhar, PRB \textbf{75}, 195110, 2007, who note the ``$i\eta$''
\emph{is} the probe self-energy, conserving only with the zero-current
condition); dephasing- vs voltage-probe (Pilgram et al., PRL \textbf{97},
066801, 2006; Beenakker \& de Jong, Physica A \textbf{230}, 219, 1996). AGF
practice uses $\eta\approx i0^+$ with $\eta\to0$ extrapolation (Sadasivam
et al., Annu. Rev. Heat Transfer \textbf{17}, 89, 2014) but, being ballistic,
does not flag the interacting defect. Our commutator formula and the
$\eta\times$asym scaling appear new.

\subsection{Recommendation}
Coherent+anharmonic conductance: refine the grid until the numerical asymmetry
is sub-dominant; run $\ge2$--$3$ converging $\eta$ and extrapolate
$G(\eta\to0)$, quoting the spread as the finite-$\eta$ band; gate convergence
on the $\Sigma$-residual and bubble balance (machine precision), not the lead
balance. Soft systems that cannot lower $\eta$ (d5a): quote the
$\eta$-floor-limited number with its band and the marginal-quasiparticle
caveat ($\gamma/\omega\sim0.5$). Reserve the B\"uttiker probe for a physically
intended incoherent channel.
