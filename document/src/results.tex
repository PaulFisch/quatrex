\chapter{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This chapter collects the quantum transport calculations: the ballistic Si/Ge
interface (\cref{sec:res_ballistic}), the force-constant inputs and their
compression (\cref{sec:res_fc}), anharmonic transport on finite silicon
nanowires (\cref{sec:res_nw}) and carbon nanotubes (\cref{sec:res_cnt}), the
transversely-periodic $q_\perp$-resolved problem and the silicon thin-film
benchmark (\cref{sec:res_qfilm}), a Si/Ge heterostructure (\cref{sec:res_hetero}),
the static loop and tadpole corrections (\cref{sec:res_scp}), self-consistent
convergence at vanishing broadening (\cref{sec:res_eta0}), the strongly
anharmonic SrTiO\textsubscript{3} test (\cref{sec:res_strong}), the cost of the
numerical approximations (\cref{sec:res_approx}), and the distributed-solver and
memory scaling of the implementation (\cref{sec:res_scaling}).
