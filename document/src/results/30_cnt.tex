\section{Carbon nanotubes}
\label{sec:res_cnt}
% ======================================================================

An armchair $(3,3)$ carbon nanotube is tested: a stiff, defect-free
$sp^2$ tube ($36$ DOF per cell, $\sim4$~\AA\ diameter). Its force
constants fit with a larger absolute residual than the silicon wires
(train/test RMSE $0.245$/$0.282$~eV\,\AA$^{-1}$, reflecting the much stiffer
$sp^2$ bonds and hence larger forces) but a clean harmonic spectrum --- no
imaginary modes, and the periodic acoustic sum rule exact (FC3 leg residual
$\sim0$, versus $0.8$ for the open wire).
% REVIEW(verified): lead channel staircase computed from the periodic dynamical matrix -- N(omega)=4 (acoustic only) up to ~7 THz, rising to a peak of 12 mid-band; dense finite-eta max transmission 11.5 (F20), d11a 4.5 (F20). The old "low-frequency plateau of ~11" was wrong: no optical branch opens below ~7 THz.
Its ballistic transmission is the integer channel staircase of the
periodic lead: exactly the four acoustic channels up to ${\sim}7$~THz (no
optical branch opens below that), rising to a peak of $12$ channels
mid-band ($\sim11.5$ in the dense finite-$\eta$ reference; compare a peak
of $\sim4.5$ for \texttt{d11a}). The SCBA converges to a tight
fixed point (heat-flow conservation $3\times10^{-5}$--$3\times10^{-4}$
across the one-to-three-cell ladder); driven to a tight
tolerance the one-cell residual reaches $9.6\times10^{-10}$ in $188$
safeguarded-Anderson iterations, giving the same conductance as the loose
($10^{-3}$) stop --- the tube has a single, well-conditioned fixed point.

\paragraph{Transport.} The physical, conserving conductance of the tube is the
$\eta\to0$ result of \cref{sec:res_eta0}: the anharmonic ratio moves
non-monotonically with temperature -- from
$G_\mathrm{anh}/G_\mathrm{ball}\approx0.75$ at $30$~K through a maximum of
$\approx0.81$ near $100$~K to $0.57$ at $300$~K -- and
deepens with device length ($0.574,0.506$ at two and three cells). We do
\emph{not} report the finite-$\eta$ ratios as the transport result: a finite
broadening systematically over-broadens the spectral function and
\emph{under}-estimates the scattering, giving a much weaker reduction (e.g.\
$0.702$ rather than $0.574$ at $300$~K, two cells), and --- as
\cref{sec:res_approx} shows --- the bias does not even extrapolate cleanly to
$\eta\to0$. The finite-$\eta$ numbers are retained only in \cref{sec:res_approx}
as a quantification of that systematic (finite-$\eta$ one-cell ratios: $0.92$
falling to $0.709$ over $30$--$300$~K at $\eta_w=0.05$~THz, but $0.810$ at
$300$~K and $\eta_w=0.206$~THz; ladder $0.810,0.702,0.694$ at one, two and
three cells, $300$~K, $\eta_w=0.206$~THz, each converged to $\le10^{-5}$ with
$\lesssim3\times10^{-4}$ heat-flow non-conservation).

\paragraph{Cutoff robustness.}  Across all eight corners of the
(FC3-magnitude, vertex-range, Green's-function-range) cutoff cube,
% REVIEW(verified): F30 -- corners span 4.09--4.96e8 about the full-coupling 4.48e8, i.e. +11/-9%, not a symmetric +-10%.
the converged $G_\mathrm{anh}$ spans $4.09$--$4.96\times10^{8}$~W\,m$^{-2}$K$^{-1}$
about the full-coupling $4.48\times10^{8}$, i.e.\ $+11/{-}9\%$
(\cref{fig:res_cnt_cutoff}). This is in contrast to the open
nanowires, where a diagonal-$G$ approximation is
catastrophic (\cref{sec:res_nw}).

\paragraph{The $(8,0)$ zigzag.} As a second, larger tube, we test
the semiconducting $(8,0)$ zigzag nanotube ($32$ atoms per cell,
the geometry of the electron-transport quatrex example). The fit is clean (test
RMSE $0.151$~eV\,\AA$^{-1}$, about half the $(3,3)$ value thanks to the larger
cell; a soft $0.0014$~THz twist, as befits the wider tube), and the ballistic
conductance behaves as expected --- it decreases with length through finite-$\eta$
coherence attenuation, and the larger tube carries more channels at lower per-area
conductance. In the dense finite-$\eta$ reference at one cell and $300$~K the
$(8,0)$ shows a slightly stronger anharmonic reduction than the $(3,3)$
($0.775$ versus $0.810$), consistent with the larger cell's greater channel count
and three-phonon phase space. The two-cell device ($192$ DOF) exceeds the dense
solver's reach, but the production $\eta\to0$ solver converges it to a fixed
% REVIEW(verified): cnt80_eta0 summary L2 has n_slabs=2 -- this is a TWO-cell number (ratio 0.7833, lead 4.1e-5, 37 iters), not one cell as previously stated.
point at $G_\mathrm{anh}/G_\mathrm{ball}=0.783$ ($300$~K, lead imbalance
$4.1\times10^{-5}$, $37$ iterations). At matched cell count this $\eta=0$
reduction is much \emph{weaker} than the $(3,3)$'s two-cell $0.574$; note,
however, that the comparison is not like-for-like in physical length --- the
$(8,0)$ period is $4.28$~\AA\ against the $(3,3)$'s $2.46$~\AA, so two cells of
the two tubes are devices of different lengths --- and we do not attribute the
difference further here. The three-cell $(8,0)$ point is running at the time of
writing.
