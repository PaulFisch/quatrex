\section{Self-consistent convergence at vanishing broadening}
\label{sec:res_eta0}
% ======================================================================

The numerical broadening $\eta$ in \cref{eq:system} regularises the contact and
device Green's functions but is not physical; a conserving anharmonic
conductance is defined only in the $\eta\to0$ limit (\cref{sub:eta_zero}). We
examined whether that limit is reachable directly, using the
extrapolation/root-finding solvers of \cref{sub:rootfinders} with the smooth
spectral window and infrared taper. The two test structures behave oppositely,
and the boundary between them is set by a single, a~priori computable quantity:
the anharmonic linewidth relative to the frequency grid.

\paragraph{CNT: a genuine $\eta=0$ fixed point.}
For the armchair (3,3) tube the $\eta=0$ SCBA converges to a true fixed point.
With the smooth window, the infrared taper, the causal (Kramers--Kronig)
retarded self-energy and plain linear or reduced-rank-extrapolation mixing at
$\eta=10^{-12}$, the two-cell device reaches a relative $\Sigma^R$ residual of
$3.8\times10^{-11}$, a lead-current imbalance
$|J_L-J_R|/|J|=3.2\times10^{-11}$, and an energy-balance residual
(\cref{eq:bubble_balance}) of $1.8\times10^{-16}$ in $49$ iterations
(\cref{fig:res_eta0_conv}, left) -- a self-consistent, current-conserving
solution at no broadening. The physical conductance is the grid-weighted heat
current $G\cdot\mathrm{d}\omega\approx 17$ (the unweighted lead sum scales with
the number of grid points and is not itself the conductance). It is insensitive
at the few-percent level to the infrared regularisation: across
$\omega_{\rm reg}=1.83$--$3.06$~THz the converged $G\cdot\mathrm{d}\omega$ varies
by $3.7\%$ and extrapolates to $17.4$ as $\omega_{\rm reg}\to0$
(\cref{fig:res_eta0_cutoff}, left), and it changes by $1\%$ between the $181$-
and $361$-point grids (right).

\paragraph{Anharmonic transport from the converged solution.} The conserving
$\eta=0$ fixed point yields a genuine inelastic conductance, and its ballistic
baseline is \emph{exactly} the Landauer channel count: at every resolved
frequency the converged ballistic $T(\omega)$ equals the integer number of lead
bands crossing $\omega$ (computed independently from the periodic dynamical
matrix; maximum deviation below $10^{-4}$ channels, peak $12=12$, zero
unitarity violations, and $T_{\rm anh}\le T_{\rm ball}$ in every bin,
\cref{fig:res_eta0_trans}). The $\eta=0$ transmission being \emph{higher} than
any finite-$\eta$ result is therefore not an artefact but the removal of one:
finite $\eta$ makes the near-edge channels artificially evanescent and absorbs
flux in the device interior, pushing $T$ \emph{below} the exact integers
(\cref{sec:res_approx}). Three-phonon scattering then removes $39\%$ of the
band-integrated transmission at two transport cells and $46\%$ at three -- the
resistive onset grows with length. The ballistic conductance is itself length-independent (the Landauer
baseline, $8.0\times10^{8}$~W\,m$^{-2}$K$^{-1}$ at two, three and four cells), so
the falling anharmonic ratio is genuine scattering, not coherence loss. The
frequency-integrated $G_\mathrm{anh}/G_\mathrm{ball}$ (\cref{fig:res_eta0_transport};
the heat conductance weights $T(\omega)$ by the thermal window $\partial n/\partial T$,
so it is not identical to the flat band-integrated suppression quoted above)
rises modestly from $0.75$ at $30$~K to $\approx 0.81$ near $100$~K and then falls
to $0.57$ at $300$~K as the three-phonon scattering rate grows with the thermal
phonon population, and it deepens with device length, $0.57\to0.51$ from two to
three transport cells at $300$~K (the four-cell anharmonic run did not close
the residual gate and is excluded; its best iterate gives ratio $0.479$ at a
lead imbalance of $1.3\times10^{-4}$). These production-NEGF values at
$\eta=10^{-12}$ are obtained with a different solver and broadening than the
dense-reference numbers of \cref{sec:res_cnt} (a monotonic $0.92\to0.709$ at
$\eta_w=0.05$~THz, one cell); they show a stronger reduction
but the same qualitative increase of anharmonic resistance with temperature and
length. The stronger reduction is the physical $\eta\to0$ limit, not an
artefact: at matched broadening the ratio falls monotonically toward this value
as $\eta\to0$, and the converged $\eta=10^{-12}$ conductance is robust to the
infrared regulariser and grid at the few-percent level while conserving at
$7\times10^{-6}$; the finite-$\eta$ systematic that separates it from a
single-broadening number is quantified in \cref{app:conservation}.

\paragraph{Infrared behaviour.}
The low-frequency end warrants separate discussion. The ballistic transmission
plateaus at the four acoustic channels of the tube,
$\mathcal{T}(\omega\to0)\to N_{\rm ac}=4$, so the heat-current density
$\hbar\omega\,I(\omega)$ tends to a constant --- the Bose factor's $1/\omega$ is
cancelled by the $\hbar\omega$ weight, the quantised-conductance plateau
$g_0=\pi^2k_B^2T/3h$ per channel (\cref{fig:res_eta0_ir}, blue;
\cref{eq:ir_plateau}). This plateau is not merely flat but \emph{quantised}: in
units of $k_B\Delta T/2\pi$ the converged ballistic heat-current density reads
$3.999$ at the first resolved bin -- the absolute quantum of thermal
conductance per channel times $N_{\rm ac}=4$, with no adjustable normalisation.
The $\eta=0$ anharmonic curve, computed here \emph{without}
the infrared taper, is instead suppressed as $\omega\to0$, with a shallow
non-monotonic dip (\cref{fig:res_eta0_ir}, red). This is \emph{not} a Bose pole in the scattering
self-energy: the bosonic fold and the linearly-vanishing acoustic spectral weight
cancel the $1/\omega$ of the bubble legs, and the bare bubble is exactly
conserving (\cref{app:conservation}). What remains is the first-bin sampling of
the genuine acoustic retarded enhancement $G^R\sim1/\omega^2$ at the soft modes,
amplified by the marginal $\eta=0$ fixed-point fluctuation of \cref{sub:eta_zero}.
Because the heat current is $\hbar\omega$-weighted this region carries little
conductance --- the converged $G\!\cdot\!\mathrm{d}\omega$ is insensitive to the
infrared regularisation at the few-percent level (\cref{fig:res_eta0_cutoff}) ---
so the accurate, conserving choice is the bare bubble, the taper serving only as a
pragmatic stabiliser removed by the $\omega_{\rm reg}\to0$ extrapolation. The
$1/\omega$ that is physical lives in the observable heat current, where the
$\hbar\omega$ weight turns it into the finite plateau, not in the bubble. The
quasi-one-dimensional acoustic linewidths of the tube~\cite{bruns_nanotube_2021}
mean the converged anharmonic infrared limit need not coincide with the full
ballistic plateau.

\paragraph{d5a: a marginal fixed point at finite grid.}
The hydrogen-passivated \texttt{d5a} wire behaves differently because of its
surface modes. The golden-rule linewidth (\cref{eq:fgr}), evaluated directly from
$\Phi^{(3)}$ with no SCBA, separates cleanly by hydrogen character
(\cref{fig:res_eta0_gamma}): the silicon acoustic and optical modes
($0$--$15$~THz) have $\Gamma_{\rm anh}\approx4$~THz, far above any feasible grid
spacing; the Si--H bending band ($\approx 16$--$21$~THz, $\sim 90\%$ hydrogen character)
has a median $\Gamma_{\rm anh}\approx 0.65$~THz but a tail down to
$\approx 0.13$~THz; and the Si--H stretch ($\approx 63$~THz) has
$\Gamma_{\rm anh}\approx 0.13$~THz but carries no heat -- its occupation is
frozen and it is masked. The sharp, heat-carrying tail of the bending band thus
straddles the grid spacing ($\mathrm{d}\omega=0.37$ and $0.18$~THz at $181$ and
$361$ points), and by \cref{eq:resolvent_gain} it renders the $\eta=0$ fixed
point marginal.

Consistent with this, the bare $\eta=0$ iteration does not converge by
contraction (\cref{fig:res_eta0_conv}, right): plain and reduced-rank mixing
limit-cycle, and the RPM of \cref{sub:rootfinders} diverges, its measured
unstable-subspace radius growing from $|\lambda(H)|\approx 28$ ($3$ unstable
modes) at $181$ points to $\approx 200$ ($5$ modes) at $361$ -- the instability
\emph{strengthens} as the grid resolves the sharp Si--H modes, confirming it is
physical rather than a discretisation artefact. Jacobian-free Newton--Krylov stays
bounded but its inner GMRES cannot reduce the Newton residual below $\sim 97\%$ of
its value at any Krylov dimension, the signature of a near-singular, indefinite
Jacobian. The fixed point is therefore \emph{marginal}: the flexural-mode
eigenvalue of the SCBA map sits at $|\lambda|\approx1$, so no iteration can drive
the self-energy residual to zero.

The transport observable, however, is well defined
(\cref{fig:res_d5a_eta0_panel}). A DC-concentrated,
grid-consistent broadening floor ($\Gamma_{\rm floor}\propto\mathrm{d}\omega^2$,
acting only on the unresolved sub-grid bins so that it vanishes in the continuum)
lifts the marginal mode just enough to enter its basin, and a warm-started
trust-region JFNK then settles there. Across the four floored JFNK variants that
reach the basin the self-energy residual floors at $0.016$--$0.032$ -- not
$10^{-10}$, the marginal mode forbidding a tight fixed point -- while the
conductance is method-invariant at the one-percent level: $G=2.613$--$2.640$
(trust-grow $2.614$, warm-start $2.613$, fixed-trust $2.627$, pseudo-transient
$2.635$, and an independent re-run of the trust-grow recipe $2.640$); the
coarser infrared-subtraction variant, whose residual floors higher
($\approx0.07$--$0.09$), gives $2.70$. The
$\Phi$-derivable bubble balance \cref{eq:bubble_balance} holds at machine
precision ($10^{-17}$--$10^{-16}$) at every iterate, while the lead-current
imbalance floors at $2.5\times10^{-3}$ for the best-conserved solver
($\le1.7\times10^{-2}$ across the family) -- the finite-broadening
ghost-reservoir term of
\cref{app:conservation}, which vanishes only as the floor (hence $\mathrm{d}\omega$)
goes to zero. The floor is a crutch to reach the basin, not a physical input:
annealing it $2\to0$ grid cells over $80$ iterations preserves the fixed point
-- the solution survives at zero floor -- with the annealed endpoint within
$7\%$ of the floored family, the difference bounded by the anneal's own looser
residual ($\approx0.1$; \cref{fig:res_d5a_eta0_panel}c). The $\eta=0$
\texttt{d5a} conductance is thus converged and conserving as a transport number,
the residual marginality -- not a missing solution -- being the honest content of
the surface-mode physics. Whether the residual floor is structural
or merely resolution-limited remains open: at $361$ points the same
floor-stabilised recipe does not re-enter the basin (the warm-started JFNK
stalls at $O(1)$ residual), so the grid-doubling comparison of the floor could
not yet be made. The boundary is in either case sharp and predictive: the
$\eta=0$ anharmonic transport problem is well posed exactly when every
heat-carrying mode is resolved by the frequency grid.
