\section{Anharmonic transport in silicon nanowires}
\label{sec:res_nw}
% ======================================================================

We study two hydrogen-passivated [100] silicon nanowires: \texttt{d5a}
($\mathrm{Si}_9\mathrm{H}_{12}$ per cell, $63$ DOF) and the wider
\texttt{d11a} ($135$ DOF). Their harmonic transport is physical
% REVIEW(verified): fresh Caroli sweep (ballistic_wires.py, 2026-07-02): d11a/d5a = 1.56--1.71 over 200--600 K, 1.64 at 300 K; "~1.5x" tightened.
(\cref{fig:res_ballistic_curves}): the ballistic conductance rises with
temperature, and \texttt{d11a} carries
$1.6$--$1.7\times$ the conductance of \texttt{d5a}.

\paragraph{Mode-resolved anharmonic physics.} A phono3py analysis of the
bulk-silicon FC3 (\cref{fig:res_modephysics}) is performed: phonon lifetimes
have a median of $4.2$~ps with a $\tau\!\propto\!\omega^{-2}$ envelope.
% REVIEW(verified): "93% by acoustic modes" made precise -- the frequency cut f<8 THz gives 93.1%, the branch-index cut gives 96.6% (phph_physics_si.py); Umklapp 0.44 is the rate-summed event fraction, the kappa-weighted fraction is 0.38 (phph_NU_gruneisen_si.py). All MFP/lifetime numbers are our own phono3py analysis, not literature.
$50\%$ of $\kappa$ is carried by phonons with mean free path $>134$~nm
and $93\%$ by modes below $8$~THz ($96.6\%$ when counted by acoustic branch
index). Umklapp accounts for $0.44$ of the scattering events at $300$~K
($0.38$ when weighted by the $\kappa$ contribution),
and the mode Gr\"uneisen parameters have mean $|\gamma|=1.04$, consistent
with the experimental Si scale
($\gamma\approx1$)~\cite{slack_thermal_1975} and validating the FC3
anharmonicity strength.
Only $1.3\%$ of bulk $\kappa$ comes from phonons with mean free path below $10$~nm. The
$\sim1$~nm nanowire cores therefore sit far below the bulk
phonon-MFP spectrum: they are mostly performing boundary scattering and
retain almost none of the bulk anharmonic-limited $\kappa$.

\paragraph{What the open-wire SCBA requires.} At physical broadening and
moderate vertex strength the SCBA converges cleanly and gives the physical
$G_\mathrm{anh}<G_\mathrm{ball}$. Two conditions are necessary for the
nanowire self-consistency to reach this physical value. First, the three-phonon
vertex must be built from an \emph{acoustic-sum-rule-projected} FC3: the raw
mass-weighted FC3 violates translational invariance on the open device and couples
the scattering vertex into the soft acoustic/twist subspace, so the unprojected
$\lambda{=}1$ SCBA diverges where the projected one converges (the projection drops
$\sim25\%$ of $\|\Phi^{(3)}\|$ and leaves the ballistic baseline unchanged). Second,
the broadening must be physical: an over-large diagnostic
$\eta_w\sim0.7$--$2$\,THz crushes the low-frequency acoustic channels that carry the
% REVIEW(verified): "two orders" understated the collapse -- the archived eta-sweep gives G_ball = 2.78e7 (eta_w 0.067) -> 1.0e6 (0.67) -> 5.5e3 (2.0) W/m^2K, i.e. factors of ~30 to ~5x10^3.
wire's heat, collapsing $G_\mathrm{ball}$ by a factor of $\sim30$ at
$\eta_w=0.67$\,THz and $\sim5\times10^{3}$ at $\eta_w=2$\,THz, and
producing the spurious $G_\mathrm{anh}>G_\mathrm{ball}$ of early sweeps; at physical
$\eta_w$ the resistive ordering is restored.

\Cref{tab:res_asr_compare} makes the projection effect quantitative on a single
representative \texttt{d5a} point (one cell, $\eta_w\approx0.11$~THz, full coupling
$\lambda=1$): the raw vertex diverges --- the residual spiking past $10^2$ in the
course of the iteration ($1.2$ at the abort), $620$ causality-%
violating frequency points, the guard aborting after ten iterations --- while the
projected vertex converges monotonically in thirty iterations to residual
$8.8\times10^{-4}$ with a bounded $\max|\Sigma^R|\approx136$~THz$^2$ and no causality
violations; the projection lowers the stacked-FC3 Frobenius norm by $25\%$ and leaves
% REVIEW(verified): "identical to seven figures" is not supported by the surviving record (the asr_compare json is purged; the archive records G_ball=2.153e7 both ways, four figures).
the ballistic conductance unchanged ($G_\mathrm{ball}=2.153\times10^{7}$ both ways).

of \cref{sec:res_approx}, not a physical anti-resistance. Across temperature the band

\paragraph{Width dependence of the soft mode.} The near-zero twist is specific to the
narrow \texttt{d5a}. The wider \texttt{d11a} has no imaginary modes and its lowest
non-translational mode sits at $1.07$~THz, a factor of $\sim40$ above
% REVIEW(verified): "Bose-enhanced infrared stiffness" -> "occupation/sensitivity"; the archived mechanism is the huge Bose factor of the near-zero twist (n_B(0.027 THz, 300 K) ~ 220) driving the SCBA's infrared sensitivity.
\texttt{d5a}'s $0.027$~THz twist, so the huge Bose occupation of a near-zero mode
($n_B\approx220$ at $300$~K for the \texttt{d5a} twist) -- and with it the infrared
sensitivity of the self-consistency -- is absent and
its SCBA is well conditioned: the finite-$\eta$ vertex-continuation converges in
$37$ iterations at $\lambda=0.3$ and stays stable to $\lambda=0.6$ (these are
low-coupling, finite-$\eta$ convergence points, not the physical conductance, which
is the $\eta\to0$ result of \cref{sec:res_eta0}). \texttt{d11a} is therefore the
better-conditioned structure for production nanowire transport. A third wire,
\texttt{d17a}, was
discarded: its force constants as fit carry $86$ imaginary modes at $\Gamma$ down to
% REVIEW(verified): "resolved by refitting" overstated -- the on-disk d17a fit is still the broken one (hiphive_fit.json: n_structures=6, unregularised least-squares, train RMSE ~1e-14 vs test RMSE 0.37 eV/A = pure overfit); no refit was performed, the wire was set aside.
$-34$~THz --- a fit-quality artefact of training on too few rattled supercells (six
configurations with an unregularised least-squares fit, train RMSE
${\sim}10^{-14}$ against test RMSE $0.37$~eV\,\AA$^{-1}$). The cure would be a
refit on more configurations, not a solver change; the refit was not pursued and
the wire was set aside --- a reminder that the harmonic
input must be vetted before any anharmonic transport run.

A self-energy-level cutoff sweep on the \texttt{d5a} and \texttt{d11a}
nanowires (\cref{fig:res_cutoffs}) quantifies the truncations: the FC3
magnitude threshold converges quickly for \texttt{d5a}
($10^{-3}\to0.7\%$ relative change in $\Sigma^<$) but the wider
\texttt{d11a} needs a tighter threshold. A diagonal-$G$ approximation inside the
bubble is catastrophic ($5\times$ for \texttt{d5a}, $30\times$ for
% REVIEW(verified): plotted d5a curve = original fit (reaps/hiphive_sinw100_d5a_vasp, diag-G 5.01x); the sc4-refit sweep survives in git (commit d2854d90, sinw100_d5a_vasp_sc4/cutoffs/cutoffs_sweep.csv: diag_G mean 76.7x) -- the ~77x is confirmed.
\texttt{d11a}; on the tighter \texttt{d5a} refit used for the transport studies
the diagonal-$G$ error grows further, to ${\sim}77\times$). The dominant cutoff
is the Green's-function range, not the vertex magnitude.

So the twist frequency itself is fit-dependent over ~2 decades; the fit-robust facts are (i) never imaginary and (ii) far softer than d11a's 1.07 THz in the canonical fits. The full three-amplitude sweep data is no longer on disk.
Performing a rattle-amplitude sweep for the \texttt{d5a} nanowire -- independently
refitting the force constants at each training amplitude ($0.015$--$0.08$~\AA) --
the twist mode stays real at every amplitude, never imaginary: the mechanical
stability of \texttt{d5a} is robust to the fit inputs. The twist \emph{frequency},
by contrast, is not a fit-robust number: the rattle-sweep refits place the lowest
non-translational mode at $0.5$--$1.0$~THz, whereas the original fit gives the
$0.027$~THz of this section and the \texttt{sc4} production refit $0.0075$~THz ---
for a quasi-Goldstone torsion this close to zero, the frequency sits at the
fits' noise floor and scatters over two decades between fits. The sweep therefore
validates the mode's realness, not a particular frequency; the SCBA conditioning
argument above rests only on the fit-robust fact that the \texttt{d5a} twist is
orders of magnitude softer than \texttt{d11a}'s $1.07$~THz.
