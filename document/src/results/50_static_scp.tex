Beyond the dynamic three-phonon bubble $\Sigma_B(\omega)$ (the bubble
self-energy $\Sigma_s$ of \cref{eq:sigma_guo}; the only term
with an imaginary part, hence the linewidth), the lowest-order diagrammatic expansion
adds two static, real, Hermitian self-energies: the quartic loop
$\Sigma_\mathrm{loop}=\tfrac12\,\Phi_4\!:\!\langle uu\rangle$ (needs FC4) and the cubic tadpole
$\Sigma_T=\Phi_3\!\cdot\!\langle u\rangle$ with
$\langle u\rangle=-\Phi_\mathrm{eff}^{-1}\,\tfrac12\Phi_3\!:\!\langle
uu\rangle$, where $\Phi_\mathrm{eff}$ is the effective (renormalised)
harmonic, i.e.\ second-order, force-constant matrix---the bare curvature
$D$ plus the static self-energy correction---(internal-coordinate
relaxation, needs FC3). We computed all
three contributions across temperature for the soft-mode
\texttt{d5a} nanowire and the stiff (3,3) carbon nanotube
(\cref{fig:res_scp_mag}). The CNT FC4 was obtained from the existing
rattled set with a short ($2.0$~\AA) quartic cutoff,
the \texttt{d5a} FC4 from its dedicated quartic refit
(\cref{ch:input_gen}).

For the CNT, the dynamic bubble dominates but not overwhelmingly:
$\max|\mathrm{Re}\,\Sigma_B|:\|\Sigma_\mathrm{loop}\|:\|\Sigma_T\|\approx
232:97:111~\mathrm{THz^2}\approx 2.4:1:1.1$
at $300$~K, all growing with $T$ as $\langle uu\rangle$ grows. For the
soft \texttt{d5a} wire every term is larger and the static corrections are
\emph{comparable} to the bubble ($\max|\mathrm{Re}\,\Sigma_B|\approx734$~THz$^2$,
$\|\Sigma_\mathrm{loop}\|\approx824$~THz$^2$ at $300$~K): the static
renormalisation is not a small correction on the soft wire.

For the CNT, the loop is a $\sim3\%$ frequency renormalisation (lowest
optical device mode $12.23\to12.03$~THz at $300$~K) that leaves both the
conductance ratio and the $\sim10^{-3}$ heat-flow conservation essentially
unchanged --- for the stiff tube it is unnecessary. For \texttt{d5a} it is
the dominant static term and it matters where the wire is hardest: at
$300$~K the loop improves the heat-flow conservation from $2.6\%$
(bubble-only) to $0.7\%$ and stiffens the soft mode
($0.88\to1.05$~THz), moving the conductance ratio from $0.85$ to $0.91$
at this one-cell, finite-$\eta$ operating point. The loop is the
physical cure for a soft mode: stiffening it lifts the propagator out of
the near-singular region. (At low temperature the picture inverts ---
bubble-only conservation at $30$~K is already $6\times10^{-4}$ and the
static corrections gain nothing --- the loop earns its cost only where
$\langle uu\rangle$ is large.)
Numerically, the relaxation $\langle u\rangle=-\Phi_\mathrm{eff}^{-1}(\dots)$
is not formed by a bare inverse. The symmetrised $\Phi_\mathrm{eff}$ is
eigendecomposed and inverted with an eigenvalue floor: only modes above a
cutoff $\max(\texttt{omega2\_floor\_rel}\cdot\lambda_\mathrm{max},\,\texttt{scp\_floor})$
are kept (a relative floor $10^{-3}$ together with an absolute
$\texttt{scp\_floor}$ in THz$^2$), giving a floored pseudo-inverse. This
eigenvalue floor---not the FC4 loop---is the code's actual cure for the
singular inversion of a soft $\Phi_\mathrm{eff}$.

Run in isolation, the tadpole breaks down on the soft wire: at $300$~K the
tadpole-only self-energy grows to $\|\Sigma_T\|=824$~THz$^2$, drives the
lowest mode imaginary ($0.88\to0.81i$~THz, i.e.\ $\omega^2=-0.66$~THz$^2$)
and the SCBA does not converge (heat-flow conservation degrading to
$4.7\%$). The optical tadpole
$T_O$ is the gradient (force on the internal coordinates) that relaxes the
internal coordinates and vanishes at the relaxed structure.
With coupled self-consistency, the quartic loop
stiffens the modes, which lowers $\langle uu\rangle$, which shrinks
$\langle u\rangle$ and hence $\Sigma_T$: run together
(as SSCHA does~\cite{tadano_first-principles_2018}) the same $300$~K point gives
a coupled tadpole of $185$~THz$^2$ ($4.5\times$ smaller than isolated) and a
stable lowest mode ($0.98$~THz). On the stiff CNT the isolated tadpole
never breaks down (it is in fact the best-conserving variant there,
$10^{-5}$--$10^{-4}$), confirming that the breakdown is a soft-mode
phenomenon, not a property of the diagram. On bulk silicon $\|\Sigma_T\|$
stays at a few percent of the optical $\omega^2$ across $100$--$600$~K and
never destabilises.
