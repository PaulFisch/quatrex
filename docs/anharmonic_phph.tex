\chapter{Anharmonic phonon-phonon scattering self-energy}
\label{ch:anharmonic_phph}

% ======================================================================
\section{Overview and scope}
\label{sec:phph_overview}
% ======================================================================

This chapter documents the addition of cubic ($3$-phonon) scattering to
the QuaTrEx phonon NEGF solver. The cubic vertex
$\Phi^{(0)}_{i\alpha,j\beta,k\gamma}$ is supplied by the existing
\texttt{phonon\_inputs} pipeline (phono3py / hiphive / DFPT). The
self-energy is built in real space without transverse periodicity (no
$\mathbf q$-mesh), respecting the existing block-tridiagonal device
layout: $N_{\rm slabs}$ transport cells, with one or several primitive
cells per transport cell, and nearest-neighbour coupling between
transport cells.

The same energy-convolution + Hilbert-transform machinery already used for
the dynamical Coulomb-screening self-energy is reused, so the only new
mathematical content beyond \S\ref{sec:phonopy_units} is the cubic
vertex contraction and its block-sparsity pattern.

% ======================================================================
\section{Cubic anharmonic Hamiltonian}
\label{sec:phph_hamiltonian}
% ======================================================================

Let $u_a$ denote a Cartesian displacement, with composite index
$a = (i,\alpha)$ ($i$ atom, $\alpha\in\{x,y,z\}$). In the
mass-weighted basis
\begin{equation}
  \tilde u_a \;=\; \sqrt{m_i}\,u_a,
\end{equation}
the harmonic + cubic lattice Hamiltonian reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phph_hamiltonian}
  H \;=\; \tfrac12\sum_{ab}\Phi^{(2)}_{ab}\,\tilde u_a \tilde u_b
        \;+\; \tfrac{1}{3!}\sum_{abc}\Phi_{abc}\,
              \tilde u_a \tilde u_b \tilde u_c
        \;+\; \mathcal O(\tilde u^4),
\end{equation}
where the mass-weighted vertex is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi_mass_weighted}
  \Phi_{abc}
  \;=\;
  \frac{\Phi^{(0)}_{i\alpha,\,j\beta,\,k\gamma}}
       {\sqrt{m_i\,m_j\,m_k}},
\end{equation}
with $\Phi^{(0)}$ the bare third-order force-constant tensor produced by
phono3py / symfc / hiphive / DFPT and stored as
\texttt{fc3.hdf5}. The vertex is fully symmetric,
$\Phi_{abc} = \Phi_{\sigma(abc)}$ for any permutation $\sigma$.

% ======================================================================
\section{Three-phonon self-energy (Migdal bubble)}
\label{sec:phph_bubble}
% ======================================================================

Let $G^{\lessgtr}_{ab}(\omega)$ denote the lesser/greater
displacement-displacement Keldysh component (the phonon Green's function
solved by \texttt{PhononSolver}). The leading $3$-phonon scattering
self-energy is the Migdal-like bubble
(Guo \emph{et al.}, Phys.\ Rev.\ B 102, 195412 (2020), Eq.\ 8):
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation}\label{eq:Sigma_pp}
  \Sigma^{\lessgtr}_{ab}(\omega)
  \;=\;
  \frac{i\hbar}{2}\sum_{c,d,e,f}
  \Phi_{acd}\,
  \bigl[G^{\lessgtr}\!*\!G^{\lessgtr}\bigr]_{cf,\,de}(\omega)\,
  \Phi_{bef},
\end{empheq}
with the $\omega$-convolution
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G_conv_G}
  \bigl[G^{\lessgtr}\!*\!G^{\lessgtr}\bigr]_{cf,\,de}(\omega)
  \;=\;
  \int\!\frac{d\omega'}{2\pi}\;
  G^{\lessgtr}_{cf}(\omega')\,
  G^{\lessgtr}_{de}(\omega-\omega').
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:Sigma_pp} is the SCBA-level closure: it is the unique
diagram with two $\Phi$ vertices and two internal phonon lines. Higher
orders (vertex corrections, $\Phi^4$ tadpole, etc.) are neglected at this
level, consistent with all reference NEGF phonon-phonon transport
implementations.

% ----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Retarded reconstruction via Hilbert transform}
\label{sec:phph_retarded}
% ----------------------------------------------------------------------

The retarded self-energy is reconstructed from the lesser/greater pair by
the Kramers--Kronig relation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phph_KK}
  \Sigma^{R}(\omega)
  \;=\;
  \tfrac12\bigl[\Sigma^{>}(\omega) - \Sigma^{<}(\omega)\bigr]
  \;+\;
  \tfrac{i}{2}\,\mathcal H\!\bigl[\Sigma^{>} - \Sigma^{<}\bigr](\omega),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal H$ is the Hilbert transform along the $\omega$-axis. We
reuse \emph{verbatim} the FFT Hilbert kernel implemented in
\texttt{coulomb\_screening/polarization.py}
(\texttt{hilbert\_transform()}), now relocated into the shared helper
\texttt{src/quatrex/core/fft\_utils.py}. The standalone reference
implementation of \S\ref{sec:phph_reference} also offers a singular
principal-value path; this is dropped in production for parity with the
Coulomb-screening side and to avoid an $\mathcal O(n_\omega^{\,2})$
reconstruction.

% ----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Symmetries enforced after each SCBA update}
\label{sec:phph_symmetries}
% ----------------------------------------------------------------------

The phonon-phonon self-energy obeys the bosonic Keldysh symmetries
\begin{align}
  \Sigma^{<}_{ab}(\omega)   &= -\bigl[\Sigma^{>}_{ba}(-\omega)\bigr]^{*},
  \label{eq:phph_bose_full_axis}\\
  \Sigma^{R}_{ab}(\omega)   &= \bigl[\Sigma^{R}_{ba}(-\omega)\bigr]^{*},
  \\
  \Sigma^{<,>}_{ab}(\omega) &= -\bigl[\Sigma^{<,>}_{ba}(\omega)\bigr]^{*}.
\end{align}
Equation~\eqref{eq:phph_bose_full_axis} is the bosonic
\emph{full-axis} symmetry; it is enforced after every SCBA update via
\texttt{DSDBSparse.symmetrize}, exactly mirroring the standalone
implementation
\texttt{anharmonic.py:\_symmetrize\_lesser\_greater}.

\section{Block-sparsity and storage of \texorpdfstring{$\Phi$}{Phi}}
\label{sec:phph_block_sparsity}
% ======================================================================

Two distinct sparsity questions arise: the sparsity of $\Phi$ itself, and
the sparsity of the resulting $\Sigma_{\rm pp}$.

\paragraph{Sparsity of $\Phi$ (block-sparse storage).}
A dense storage of $\Phi$ is
$\mathcal O(N_{\rm dof}^{3})$ in memory, which is prohibitive for the
sizes targeted by QuaTrEx (e.g.\ a $54$-atom Si transport cell already
gives $162^{3}\approx 4.3$\,M complex entries). Physically, $\Phi$ is
strictly local in real space — non-zero only when atoms $a$, $b$, $c$ all
fall within a short-range cutoff. We therefore store $\Phi$ as a
\emph{block-sparse triplet container} keyed by transport-cell triplets:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi_blocks}
  \Phi
  \;\equiv\;
  \bigl\{\,(I,J,K)\;\mapsto\;
          \Phi^{(I,J,K)}\in\mathbb C^{b_I\times b_J\times b_K}\,\bigr\},
\end{equation}
with $|I-J|,|I-K|,|J-K|\le 1$ enforced at build time. Here $b_I$ is the
DOF size of transport cell $I$. The contraction
\eqref{eq:Sigma_pp} loops only over present keys and never materialises a
dense tensor. The same dictionary is the on-disk format produced by
the (planned) sparse FC3 writer in \texttt{phonon\_inputs}.

\paragraph{Sparsity of $\Sigma_{\rm pp}$ (block-tridiagonal).}
The 3-phonon bubble can in principle populate up to $|I-J|\le 2$ for
nearest-neighbour-supported $\Phi$. The user's transport-cell
construction allows this to be controlled: by enlarging the
\emph{primitive-cells-per-transport-cell} factor, all relevant $\Phi$
triplets fall inside a single transport cell, and the resulting
$\Sigma_{\rm pp}$ is naturally block-tridiagonal — matching the existing
QuaTrEx sparsity pattern. We project
$\Sigma_{\rm pp}$ onto the existing
\texttt{DSDBSparse} sparsity pattern and emit a one-time warning if the
projection drops more than $1\%$ of the Frobenius norm.

\section{Energy-convolution algorithm}
\label{sec:phph_algorithm}
% ======================================================================

The convolution \eqref{eq:G_conv_G} is computed via FFT along the
$\omega$-axis, zero-padded to length
$n_{\rm fft}=2n_\omega-1$ to suppress wrap-around. The cubic vertex is
$\omega$-independent, so the contraction is performed in the FFT domain
in a single sweep — porting the layout of
\texttt{anharmonic.py:\_compute\_phph\_self\_energy\_finite}:
\begin{align}
  \tilde G^{\lessgtr}(\omega)
    &\;=\; \mathrm{FFT}_\omega\bigl[G^{\lessgtr}\bigr]
        \quad\text{(zero-padded)},
        \label{eq:phph_step_FFT}\\
  A_{a c d}(\omega)
    &\;=\; \sum_{e}\;\Phi^{(I)}_{a c e}\;\tilde G^{\lessgtr}_{e d}(\omega),
        \label{eq:phph_step_A}\\
  B_{a b d}(\omega)
    &\;=\; \sum_{c}\;A_{a c d}(\omega)\;\tilde G^{\lessgtr}_{c b}(\omega),
        \label{eq:phph_step_B}\\
  \widehat\Sigma^{\lessgtr}_{a b}(\omega)
    &\;=\; \frac{i\hbar}{2}\;
           \sum_{d}\;B_{a b d}(\omega)\;\Phi^{(J)}_{b\,\cdot\,d},
        \label{eq:phph_step_S}\\
  \Sigma^{\lessgtr}(\omega)
    &\;=\; \mathrm{IFFT}_\omega\bigl[\widehat\Sigma^{\lessgtr}\bigr]
           \cdot \frac{d\omega}{2\pi}\,.
        \label{eq:phph_step_IFFT}
\end{align}
The outer loop is over the keys $(I,J,K)$ of the
$\Phi$-block dictionary; \eqref{eq:phph_step_A}--\eqref{eq:phph_step_S}
are dense per block-triplet but small. The implementation reuses
\texttt{qttools.fft.fft\_convolve} (already imported by
\texttt{coulomb\_screening/polarization.py}).

\section{Units and prefactor}
\label{sec:phph_units}
% ======================================================================

The standalone reference \texttt{anharmonic.py} works in
$\mathrm{THz}^{2}$. The QuaTrEx solver loads dynamical matrices in
$(\mathrm{rad}/\mathrm{s})^{2}$ via
\texttt{quatrex\_writer.py}, but the energy axis on the
\texttt{PhononSolver} side is conventionally in eV (i.e.\
$\hbar\omega$). We therefore adopt one consistent
internal unit system: $\omega$ in eV, $\Phi$ pre-converted to
$\mathrm{eV}^{2}$, and the prefactor in
\eqref{eq:Sigma_pp} reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phph_prefactor}
  \frac{i\hbar}{2}\;\frac{d\omega}{2\pi}
  \;\xrightarrow[\text{natural units, }\hbar=1]{}\;
  \frac{i}{4\pi}\,d\omega.
\end{equation}
The conversion table from $\mathrm{THz}^{2}$ to $\mathrm{eV}^{2}$ is
already present in \texttt{phonon\_inputs/constants.py} and is reused at
FC3 load time; a unit test pins the round-trip against
\texttt{anharmonic\_transmission\_finite} on a small toy.

\section{Integration into the SCBA loop}
\label{sec:phph_scba}
% ======================================================================

The SCBA driver \texttt{src/quatrex/core/scba.py} already iterates
\begin{equation*}
  G^{\lessgtr,R}\;\xrightarrow{\text{Dyson}}\;
  \Sigma^{\lessgtr,R}_{\text{int}}\;\xrightarrow{\text{mix}}\;G^{\lessgtr,R},
\end{equation*}
sequentially calling each interaction's
\texttt{ScatteringSelfEnergy.compute()}. The new
\texttt{SigmaPhononPhonon.compute(g\_lesser, g\_greater, out=(...))}
plugs into the empty hook
\texttt{\_compute\_phonon\_phonon\_interaction()} (currently
\texttt{pass}) and produces an additive contribution to the
\texttt{(sigma\_lesser, sigma\_greater, sigma\_retarded)} buffers. The
\texttt{PhononSolver.solve(...)} side is unchanged: it consumes
$\Sigma^{\lessgtr,R}$ verbatim.

The trigger is the existing knob
\texttt{config.phonon.model = "negf"} (currently raises
\texttt{NotImplementedError}); enabling it requires \texttt{fc3\_path} to
point at a valid \texttt{fc3.hdf5}.

\section{Verification and reference parity}
\label{sec:phph_reference}
% ======================================================================

The standalone implementation
\texttt{phonon/input\_calc/phonon\_inputs/anharmonic.py:643--691}
(\texttt{\_compute\_phph\_self\_energy\_finite}) serves as the numerical
reference. We pin parity at the level of $\Sigma^{\lessgtr,R}$ in a
unit test
\texttt{tests/quatrex/phonon/test\_sse\_phonon\_phonon.py}, requiring
\begin{equation*}
  \frac{\bigl\|\Sigma^{<}_{\text{quatrex}}-\Sigma^{<}_{\text{ref}}\bigr\|_{\infty}}
       {\bigl\|\Sigma^{<}_{\text{ref}}\bigr\|_{\infty}}
  \;<\;10^{-6}
\end{equation*}
on a 4-atom toy with FC3 generated by
\texttt{phonon\_inputs/dfpt.py}. Energy conservation
$|J_L - J_R| / (|J_L|+|J_R|) < 10^{-2}$ and the bosonic
symmetry~\eqref{eq:phph_bose_full_axis} are checked at every SCBA
iteration.
