## Physical and numerical reference

The target is the cubic-bubble self-consistent Born approximation defined by
the supplied Si FC2 and FC3 model.  The certification state uses a mean
temperature of 300 K, zero device and contact broadening, causal FFT
reconstruction of the retarded self-energy and the complete spatial output
generated by the retained Green-function band.  Low-frequency masks, floors,
tapers, pole corrections, centre-of-mass subtraction and static cubic or
quartic terms are off.  A finite run is certified only when its self-energy
residual, lead balance and internal-current spread pass together.

The old run archive supplies no converged reference with these settings.  Its
frequency windows stop at 15, 20 or 28 THz, 55 of 61 historical records use
the half-retarded approximation, and the legacy self-energy sparsity pins the
output to the grouped BTD band.  The present campaign therefore starts again
on L5 and L10 before extending the length.

## Primitive contraction inside grouped Dyson blocks

Let grouped Dyson block (B_I) contain (c_I) primitive Si cells of six
degrees of freedom.  The selected Green matrix for a grouped pair is sliced
without copying,

\[
  G_{ij}(\omega,q)
  = G_{B(i)B(j)}(\omega,q)[s_i,s_j],
\]

where (s_i) is the six-row slice of primitive cell (i).  The cubic vertex
remains a dictionary of primitive blocks
(\Phi_{i k_1 k_2}\in\mathbb C^{6\times6\times6}).  Each primitive bubble
term is evaluated with the existing dense or decomposed FC3 kernel and is
accumulated into the corresponding slices of the grouped self-energy.  The
Dyson layout and the FC3 execution layout are consequently independent.

For an FC3 transport span (p) and a retained primitive Green band (b), a
nonzero term satisfies

\[
 |k_1-i|\leq p,\qquad |k_2-i|\leq p,
 \qquad |k_1-k'_1|\leq b,\qquad |k_2-k'_2|\leq b,
\]

and the right vertex requires

\[
 |k'_1-j|\leq p,\qquad |k'_2-j|\leq p.
\]

The triangle inequality gives

\[
 |i-j|\leq 2p+b.
\]

The production index builder enumerates the finite-device terms and records
the largest distance actually attained.  It raises if a generated primitive
pair lies beyond adjacent grouped blocks.  No generated shell is silently
discarded.  For nearest-neighbour Si (p=1), the first L5 candidate (b=3)
has the formal span five and the finite film attains distance four.  A single
30-DOF Dyson block therefore contains its complete L5 output.

The decomposed vertex has primitive rank (R), external factors
(D\in\mathbb R^{6\times R}) and q-resolved contracted factors of the same
primitive width.  The microblock implementation does not lift these factors
to rank (cR).  Its main stored vertex cost remains

\[
 O\!\left(R[6+2N_q n_{\rm off}6]\right),
\]

independent of the number of primitive cells grouped for Dyson.  The Gram
ring replaces dense primitive triple contractions by skinny Gram matrices and
an (R\times R) Hadamard product.  Its working memory scales as
(O(N_q N_\tau R^2)) and is bounded by tau chunking.  This is why rank 128 is
about four times the rank-64 ring cost while both are far cheaper than the
dense q-folded vertex.

## Contact construction for the L5 certification bed

Grouping all five primitive cells gives one finite Dyson block.  The two
periodic lead couplings are absent from the finite block matrix, so the solver
recovers the (+1) and (-1) FC2 transport blocks from the unit-cell input.
It constructs the two open-boundary self-energies separately, uses their sum
in the Dyson equation and retains the two separate Keldysh triples for the
left and right Meir-Wingreen currents.  Treating the contacts separately is
essential.  Aliasing them into one contact would make a one-block film appear
conserving by construction.

## Dense and decomposed vertex gate

The q=9 factor files are independent INDSCAL fits.  Their stored bulk-vertex
errors are (5.7217\times10^{-4}) at rank 64 and
(3.5053\times10^{-4}) at rank 128.  Reconstruction of sampled q-folded
primitive blocks agrees with the dense vertex at the same level.  The real Si
transport support contains seven offset pairs,

```text
(-1,-1) (-1,0) (0,-1) (0,0) (0,1) (1,0) (1,1)
```

and excludes the two opposite-corner pairs.  The Gram contraction decomposes
this Boolean support into exact rectangles before summing its line Grams, so
it does not manufacture either missing corner.

The first batched dense production probe revealed a separate two-dimensional
momentum-index error in the new microblock reference.  It formed the external
flat index as `(q1 + q2) % Nq`.  This is valid in one dimension but carries
between the two transverse coordinates on a (9\times9) mesh.  The resulting
dense self-energy differed from the correct Gram convolution by 30 to 45 per
cent.  Reconstructing the rank-64 vertex and feeding it to the same dense
kernel agreed with that incorrect dense result to (5.59\times10^{-4}),
which isolated the problem from the factor fit.

Commit `8d338692` replaces flat addition with the inverse of the stored map
(q_2=q_{\rm diff}(Q,q_1)).  A terminal-group, non-Cartesian-support test now
compares dense, batched dense, reconstructed-factor and Gram paths on a true
two-dimensional mesh.  All agree to the roundoff gate.  Dense results from
job 4555164 are consequently labelled invalid and are not used as an oracle.
Jobs through 4555202 also predate deployment of commit `8d338692` on Alps,
despite their campaign names, and remain superseded diagnostics.  The first
deployed two-dimensional q-map oracle is job 4555241.

The deployed dense comparison closes the frozen self-energy gate.  Relative
errors for the independently fitted rank-64 factors are
(5.591\times10^{-4}) in lesser, (4.222\times10^{-4}) in greater and
(3.828\times10^{-4}) in retarded self-energy.  The corresponding rank-128
errors are (6.712\times10^{-4}), (2.837\times10^{-4}) and
(2.346\times10^{-4}).  Both ranks are below the one per cent acceptance
limit.  Rank 128 introduces only (2.10\times10^{-6}) additional normalised
lesser negativity relative to a dense reference negativity of
(2.65\times10^{-3}); its anti-Hermiticity defect is
(1.46\times10^{-15}).

The factor execution has two independent roundoff checks.  On a GH200 the
rank-128 Gram result agrees with a dense reconstruction of the same factors to
(1.25\times10^{-15}).  The one-rank and four-rank Gram self-energies agree to
(2.7\times10^{-14}).  The remaining factor acceptance conditions concern the
change in converged conductance and spectral current.  They cannot be inferred
from a ballistic iteration-zero probe and are tested after the interacting L5
fixed point is certified.

At 41 points over 0 to 40 THz on one GH200, the measured ring times are
529.13 s for the dense batched q-fold, 4.46 s for rank 64 and 16.80 s for
rank 128.  Peak CuPy memory is 51.74, 10.60 and 18.17 GB respectively.  The
dense reference is affordable as a frozen certification oracle but not as a
long-film SCBA baseline.  Rank 128 remains economical if it passes the final
gate; rank 64 is retained as the faster comparison.

## Current quadrature correction

The first uniform-grid probes exposed an older reporting defect.  The stored
heat current used (\sum_n\omega_n I_n) on a uniform grid and included the
cell width only on a nonuniform grid.  The 0.25 and 1 THz probes therefore
reported currents in an almost exact ratio of four although they represented
the same harmonic state.  The constant width cancels from a conservation
ratio but does not cancel from an absolute current or conductance.

Commit `02cd9402` applies the existing per-bin frequency cell widths on every
grid.  The physical observable is now

\[
 J = \sum_{n,q} \Delta\omega_n\,\omega_n I_{nq},
\]

while lead balance, internal spread and bubble balance retain their numerical
values on a fixed uniform grid.  All production conductance convergence tests
start after this correction.  Older absolute current values in this document
are diagnostic raw sums and are not part of the length curve.

## Certification order

The active order is fixed to avoid a favourable but under-resolved long run.

1. Compare corrected dense, rank-64 and rank-128 frozen L5 self-energies.
2. Verify one-rank and four-rank numerical parity, then converge the L5 fixed
   point at (b=3), 0 to 40 THz, 0.25 THz spacing and q=9.
3. Sweep the complete L5 primitive band, then L10 bands 2, 3, 4, 5 and the
   full range until two successive refinements change conductance by no more
   than 0.2 per cent.
4. Refine frequency extent, spacing, q mesh, SCBA residual and temperature
   difference one at a time on L5, repeating the selected gates on L10.
5. Hold the certified physical configuration fixed for L5, L10, L20 and L30.

Every submitted job is recorded in
[`daint_normal_ledger.md`](../scripts/daint_normal_ledger.md).  Failed,
superseded and nonconverged artifacts remain in the run census with their
status.  They are not silently replaced by a transient with better current
balance.

## Fixed-point checkpoint semantics

The first zero-broadening L5 spatial sweep used linear damping 0.1.  All four
bands reduced the residual initially and then entered a growing soft-mode
orbit.  Ordinary Anderson depths two and five did not stabilise that orbit.
Their conservation diagnostics remained small, but a conserved transient is
not a self-consistent solution and is not accepted as a result.

This sweep exposed an independent error in the campaign checkpoint wrapper.
The callback read the residual left by iteration (n-1) before evaluating the
current residual, then saved the raw map value (F[\Sigma_n]).  It therefore
attached the wrong residual to the wrong state.  For example, the L5 (b=3)
file labelled with residual (5.986\times10^{-2}) restarted at
(8.486\times10^{-2}).  Commit `4f5b96f8` evaluates the current residual first
and saves the input iterate (\Sigma_n) held in the previous-self-energy
buffer.  Subsequent minimum-residual checkpoints are consequently states of
the reported SCBA functional, not off-by-one raw outputs.  The affected files
remain useful only as explicitly labelled starting guesses.

## Zero-broadening fixed-point continuation

The corrected L5 runs make a distinction that current conservation alone
cannot provide. Linear damping at full cubic strength reduces the relative
retarded residual at first, but then enters a stable orbit. A representative
0.25 THz run reached a best correctly checkpointed residual of
\(7.73\times10^{-2}\), with a lead-current difference of order
\(10^{-4}\). The small current difference does not certify the state because
the Dyson and bubble equations are still far from self-consistency. Anderson,
reduced-rank extrapolation, Broyden and reduced-projection trials did not
remove this orbit.

A matrix-free Newton--Krylov implementation was added as a diagnostic and as
a possible production solver. Its merit function is the absolute infinity
norm of the complete lesser, greater and retarded fixed-point residual. A
relative norm whose denominator changed at every trial had previously
accepted non-descent steps. The corrected line search rejects such steps,
reduces the trust radius after rejection and permits it to recover only after
accepted descent. On the 0.125 THz grid the method decreases the relative
residual from \(1.17\times10^{-1}\), but each Newton step requires several
complete SCBA maps. It is therefore retained only if its contraction rate
justifies this cost.

The principal residual is not an unresolved acoustic pole. Frequency-resolved
diagnostics place its maximum near 23.5 THz on the 0.25 THz grid and between
27 and 28 THz on the 0.125 THz grid. These frequencies lie in the
combination-frequency sector of the cubic bubble. Refining the grid moves the
largest component but does not by itself establish a fixed point.

Fixed-coupling continuation supplies a better-conditioned route to the same
physical equation. If the primitive cubic vertex is multiplied by \(s\), the
bubble self-energy scales initially as \(s^2\). A converged state at \(s_a\)
therefore provides the initial estimate

\[
  \Sigma_{s_b}^{(0)} = (s_b/s_a)^2\Sigma_{s_a}.
\]

The scale is not a regularisation of a reported calculation. Intermediate
states are continuation points and only \(s=1\) is physical. The first L5
point at \(s=0.25\), with zero broadening and every optional mask, taper, pole
and centre-of-mass correction disabled, converged in 20 evaluations to a
relative retarded residual of \(9.44\times10^{-5}\). Its internal-current
spread was \(5.46\times10^{-8}\) and its normalised bubble energy imbalance
was \(1.53\times10^{-8}\). The current at this deliberately weakened
interaction is not used in the length curve. Continuation through
\(s=0.5\), \(0.75\) and \(1\) is the active certification path. Run metadata
records the vertex scale so that no intermediate state can be mistaken for a
physical result.

The first submitted continuation jobs after the quarter-strength point used
descriptive environment names for the retarded method, mixer and vertex
scale. The study driver still recognised only its older compact names. Jobs
4555513, 4555533, 4555542 and 4555546 therefore inherited the TOML values:
full vertex strength, half-retarded reconstruction and linear mixing 0.1.
They are superseded solver diagnostics and none of their currents or residuals
enters a continuation result. Commit `e00c15b8` normalises the readable names
at the driver boundary and raises on a conflicting pair. Focused tests cover
the aliases used by the campaign. Subsequent jobs also pass the compact names
directly and their startup log and saved metadata are checked before their
first state is accepted.

The first corrected runs at scales 0.375 and 0.5 converged and saved their
scalar and spectral observables, but their launch commands omitted the
separate distributed self-energy output paths. The resulting measurements
remain valid continuation diagnostics, while jobs 4555585 and 4555586 failed
before their first iteration because no restart arrays existed. Commit
`8f4b288c` adds an opt-in restartability assertion. A campaign launch marked
`QX_REQUIRE_RESTARTABLE=1` now fails before constructing the solver unless it
specifies the final self-energy, best self-energy and live-best checkpoint.

The guarded half-strength repeat, job 4555593, converged in 17 evaluations.
Its relative retarded residual was \(7.87\times10^{-5}\), the lead current was
148.011503 in the driver's current units, the internal-current spread was
\(8.03\times10^{-8}\), and the normalised bubble imbalance was
\(5.17\times10^{-8}\).  The final and best four-rank self-energy slices were
both preserved.  This is the first continuation point for which the scalar
record, spectral current and restart state all survive together.  It remains
classified as superseded because \(s=0.5\) is not the physical vertex.

Writing every improved live-best state is inexpensive on L5 but scales poorly
with length.  The distributed L5 final and best states together occupy about
1.1 GB; the same selected-block layout grows approximately linearly with the
number of groups.  `QX_SIGMA_BEST_LIVE_STRIDE` therefore controls only the
filesystem write cadence.  Its default value one preserves the earlier
behaviour.  A larger value still writes the first best state, every selected
iteration and the converged state, while the exact best state is retained in
memory and written at normal shutdown.  This changes no SCBA arithmetic and
will be used for the longer films.
